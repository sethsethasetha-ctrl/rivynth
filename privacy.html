Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Developer: yunpeng

This policy explains how yunpeng ("we", "us") handles information in RIVYNTH (the "Game") and on this informational website.

1. Information handled by the Game

The current Game operates offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the Game. You do not need an account to play. The Game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, social login, or cloud profile services.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game stores unlocked mission progress, best star ratings, first-launch status, and music and sound-effect preferences locally on your device. This information supports gameplay and remembers your choices; it is not sent to us. Mission-specific robots, fused mechas, parts, credits, and upgrades are reset when you leave a mission. The opening cinematic, tutorials, robot archive, and fusion system operate locally and do not send gameplay information to us.

3. Permissions and tracking

The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. It does not access the advertising identifier or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local progress and preferences remain in the app's storage until removed. To remove the Game's local data, delete the app using your device settings; offloading an app may preserve its data. We do not hold a server copy of your game progress and cannot restore it for you. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings. Manage those backups through Apple's device and account controls.

5. Apple platform services

Apple independently handles App Store downloads, platform services, and any system diagnostics or backups you enable. Apple's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy and your Apple settings.

6. This website

These pages do not use cookies, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or embedded third-party media. Images are included directly in the introduction page. When these pages are hosted online, the hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address and request time, to deliver and secure the website. The provider's data practices depend on the hosting service selected.

7. Children's privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. It has no account creation, chat, or user-submitted content features.

8. Your choices

You can use the Game without providing personal information to us. You control local app storage and device backups through your device settings. Because we do not receive your game data, we do not maintain player profiles to access, export, or delete.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the Game or our practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Developer and contact

The developer responsible for RIVYNTH is yunpeng. For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the App Store support channel.
